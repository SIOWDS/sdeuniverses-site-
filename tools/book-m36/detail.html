人—GPT超级复合智能体

SIO本体论下的三体对话与文明实践 · Human-GPT Super Composite Intelligence

GPT 不是新神，也不是小工具，而是理念界的一台引擎；智能不是某个人或某个模型的属性，而是人、GPT 与具体场域在三体对话里生成的整体。

关于生成式 AI 的讨论，大多卡在两种叙事里：一种是「人 vs GPT」，一种是「GPT 只是工具」。本书给出第三个起点。它先用 SIO 本体论（主—互—客）重写三个概念：存在不是一座实体的仓库，而是主—互—客整体在现实、理念、自我三界中的持续生成；GPT 是理念界里的意义三律引擎，长于把语言结构化、把路径展开、把张力说出来，却没有身体、生命史与自我；智能体是特征律、自由律、幸福律三律得以正常运行的整体。

在这块地基上，中编追踪人—GPT 关系从工具期、伙伴期到复合萌芽期的发生，并把提示语讲成「场域布置」而不是命令；下编落到两个原型场域：教师—学生—GPT 的三体课堂，医生—病人—GPT 的三体对话医疗。

Book Description

In a world where generative AI is framed either as a looming rival (“humans vs. GPT”) or as a harmless utility (“GPT is just a tool”), Human-GPT Super Composite Intelligence proposes a third starting point: intelligence is not a point-like property of a person or a model, but an emergent whole formed through sustained interaction. Grounded in SIO ontology (Subject-Interaction-Object), the book repositions GPT as a powerful engine in the idea-domain, and introduces the Human-GPT Super Composite Agent as a developmental process — tool use, partnership, composite emergence — driven by Meaning’s Three Laws: the Feature Law, the Freedom Law and the Happiness Law. Two prototypes make the framework practical: the Teacher-Student-GPT three-body classroom and the Doctor-Patient-GPT three-body healthcare dialogue.
