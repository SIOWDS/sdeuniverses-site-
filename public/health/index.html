健康专栏Health

疾病不是被发现的故障，而是身体在特征纠缠中「如何发生、如何自持、如何锁死」。

Illness is not a defect to be discovered — it is how the body occurs, self-sustains, and locks in through entanglement.

专栏筹建中 · 内容陆续上线Column in the making · essays landing soon

发现范式把病看成一个已经在那里、等着被找到的坏零件。SDE 健康发生学换一个问题：这套失衡为何如此发生、又如何把自己稳定成一个回不去的状态？从慢性炎症到肿瘤，答案往往不在某个单点，而在差异序列被锁死的方式里。

The discovery paradigm treats disease as a broken part already there, waiting to be found. SDE health genesis asks a different question: why does this imbalance occur as it does, and how does it stabilise into a state that cannot roll back? From chronic inflammation to tumours, the answer often lies not in a single point but in how a sequence of difference gets locked in.

五诊 · 看的是发生Five readings of occurrence

不止看指标，更读身体如何一步步把自己走进当前的稳态。

Beyond metrics — reading how the body walked itself, step by step, into its present steady state.

癌症作为发生学问题Cancer as a genesis question

不是「哪里坏了」，而是「为何这套增殖能自我供养、并锁死退路」。

Not “what broke” but “why this proliferation feeds itself and seals every exit.”

命运即长期稳定化Fate as long-run stabilisation

健康不是一次修复，而是让身体的长期纠缠稳定在能持续再发生的一侧。

Health is not a one-off repair but keeping the body's long entanglement on the side that keeps re-occurring.
