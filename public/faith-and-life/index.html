枯干、皈依、坚忍、盼望——都不是主体某个部件的故障，而是发生如何自持、如何失步。

Dryness, conversion, perseverance, hope — not a broken part of the self, but how genesis sustains itself, and how it falls out of step.

专栏 · 首篇上线Column · first essay live

同一双看「事物如何发生」的眼睛，转向信仰与人生。这里不问「你信得够不够牢」，而问：那个曾把你击穿的现场，你上一次重新踏进去，是什么时候？信仰不是心里存放的静态物，而是身体对一次发生现场的周期性、功能等价的感官再进入依赖——本栏目就沿着这条线，重新解剖枯干、皈依、坚忍与盼望。

The same eye for how things come to be, turned toward faith and life. The question here is not "do you believe firmly enough," but: when was the last time you stepped back into the scene that once pierced you through?

首篇 · 信仰维持First · Faith Maintenance

成瘾于圣：信仰维持的再发生依赖假说

Addicted to the Sacred: The Recurrence-Dependence Hypothesis of Faith Maintenance

一位每日读经、从不生疑的信徒，为什么会在某个清晨发现祷告悉数滑落、经文只剩油墨？枯干不是信心软弱、意志懈怠或暗藏的罪，而是 S、D、E 三维同步性的崩解。本文提出「再发生依赖」，以特蕾莎与路易斯为两端判例，正面切割涂尔干、柯林斯、韦伯与十字若望的黑夜，并给出一条以「出口三维回写」而非「入口情绪强度」判定再发生的双分离预测。

Why does a believer who reads scripture daily and never doubts wake one morning to find prayer sliding off an invisible dome? Dryness is not weak faith or hidden sin, but a desynchronization of the S/D/E triad. The essay proposes "recurrence-dependence," cuts cleanly against Durkheim, Collins, Weber and John of the Cross, and offers a double-dissociation prediction judged at the exit, not the entrance.

What this column collects

信仰的发生学
The genesis of faith
皈依、枯干、坚忍——把它们从「灵性状态」重述为可诊断、可裁决的三维发生事件。
Conversion, dryness, perseverance — recast from "spiritual states" into diagnosable, decidable events of genesis.
意义与人生
Meaning and life
意义感为何会磨损、如何被重新点燃；把「持有一个信念」还原为「反复发生的事件」。
Why a sense of meaning wears thin and how it is rekindled; from "holding a belief" to "a recurring event."
可裁决的追问
Decidable inquiry
每一篇都尽量交出自己的「死法」——一个能被现实撞回来的预测或判例，而非无法反驳的漂亮话。
Each essay tries to name how it could die — a prediction reality can strike back at, not an irrefutable flourish.

本栏目的文章多由《问对WDS》对话凝成、再经增订，署名王德生。它们对哲学、社会学、神学与心理学文献的引述均为义释；对信仰经验的讨论力求描述其发生结构，而非替任何人裁断信仰的真伪。

Essays here are often distilled from《问对WDS》dialogues and then expanded, authored by Wang Desheng. Citations are paraphrase, not direct quotation; the aim is to describe the structure of religious experience, not to adjudicate anyone's faith.
