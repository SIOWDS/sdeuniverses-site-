有些判断只能被写成形象

Some Judgements Can Only Be Written as Form

论证要求读者同意，形象要求读者置身其中。这三个栏目处理的是同一道门槛：一个判断在什么条件下必须离开论证、变成一幅画、一段故事、或一整套文明配置里的做法。

An argument asks you to agree; a form asks you to stand inside it. These three columns work one threshold: when a judgement must leave argument behind and become a picture, a story, or a whole civilisational practice.
