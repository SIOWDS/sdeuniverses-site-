李约瑟问：近代科学为什么没有在中国发生？这个问题问了快一百年，答案大多是发现学式的清点——缺逻辑、缺实验、缺制度。这个专栏换一个问法：创新本来是如何发生的？道、儒、佛、汉语这套文明配置里，什么在助产新东西的诞生，什么在把它锁死？把传统当发生读，它就不再是需要被现代化甩掉的旧物，而是一套还在运行、可以被重新接通的发生装置。

Needham asked: why did modern science not arise in China? For a century the answers have been discovery-style inventories — missing logic, missing experiment, missing institutions. This column asks differently: how does innovation occur in the first place? Within the configuration of Dao, Confucianism, Buddhism and the Chinese language, what midwifes the new, and what locks it down? Read as occurrence, the tradition stops being baggage and becomes a genesis-engine still running, waiting to be reconnected.

伍V 中医与生命科学TCM & Life Science

Chinese medicine is neither pre-scientific residue nor mysticism — read as genesis, its meridians, qi and syndromes encode a classical model of how the body self-sustains, falls into disorder, and returns to order, now illuminating and illuminated by inflammation, microbiome and mind-body research.

陆VI AI时代的中华文明再创新Renewing Chinese Civilization in the AI Era

The deepest breath of Chinese civilization is "genesis": letting the new grow from the cracks. Wired into AI, the tradition becomes the missing engine of creation for this generation of intelligence.
