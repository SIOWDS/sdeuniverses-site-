Human-GPT Super Composite Intelligence
碳基—硅基协作的新智能范式——让机器补人的枚举之力，人守机器进不来的两端。（Human-GPT Super Composite Intelligence，ISBN 978-1-970820-32-4）
内 容 简 介

在一个把生成式 AI 要么框定为逼近的对手（「人类 vs GPT」）、要么框定为无害工具（「GPT 不过是个工具」）的世界里，本书提出第三个起点：智能不是一个人或一个模型的点状属性，而是经由持续互动形成的涌现整体。承 SIO 本体论之脉，本书主张：「存在」不是一座孤立实体的仓库，而是 S—I—O 的一个呼吸着的统一体，在现实、理念、自我三界中持续成形。

展 开 · 这本书在讲什么

从这个地基出发，作者为 GPT 重新定位：它不是一个有待膜拜的新「主体」，也不是一个插进旧工作流的单纯「客体」，而是理念界里一台强大的引擎——擅长结构化语言、拓展概念路径、帮张力变得可言说。然而 GPT 缺一具身体、一段生命史、一个自我；只有当它被嵌入人的 SIO 结构，它才真正具有转化性。本书引入「人—GPT 超级复合智能体」，作为一个有明确阶段的发展过程——工具使用、伙伴关系、复合涌现——由意义三律驱动：特征律（结构如何从混沌中涌现）、自由律（路径如何变得可见、可选、被点燃）、幸福律（张力如何被转化释放，而非被压制或爆发式排放）。

两个高风险的原型让框架落地：师—生—GPT 的「三体课堂」，与医—患—GPT 的「三体医疗对话」——教育与医疗在此被重新设计为有结构、可问责、护住意义的系统，可测量、可训练、有伦理根基。这既是一部技术哲学，也是一本建造「负责任、增益生命的人—AI 共存」的实操手册——在这里，真、自由与幸福可以被一同守护，而不必彼此交换。

内 容 简 介 · BOOK DESCRIPTION

In a world where generative AI is framed either as a looming rival ("humans vs. GPT") or as a harmless utility ("GPT is just a tool"), Human-GPT Super Composite Intelligence proposes a third starting point: intelligence is not a point-like property of a person or a model, but an emergent whole formed through sustained interaction. Grounded in SIO ontology (Subject-Interaction-Object), the book argues that "existence" is not a warehouse of isolated entities, but a breathing unity of S-I-O that continuously forms across three domains-reality, the realm of ideas, and the self. From this foundation, the authors reposition GPT: not a new "subject" to be worshiped, and not a mere "object" to be plugged into old workflows, but a powerful engine in the idea-domain-excellent at structuring language, expanding conceptual paths, and helping tension become speakable.

Yet GPT lacks a body, a life-history, and selfhood; it becomes genuinely transformative only when embedded into human SIO structures. The book introduces the "Human-GPT Super Composite Agent" as a developmental process with distinct stages-tool use, partnership, and composite emergence-driven by Meaning\'s Three Laws: the Feature Law (how structure emerges from chaos), the Freedom Law (how paths become visible, chosen, and ignited), and the Happiness Law (how tension is transformed and released rather than suppressed or explosively discharged). Two high-stakes prototypes make the framework practical: the Teacher-Student-GPT "three-body classroom," and the Doctor-Patient-GPT "three-body healthcare dialogue," where education and medicine are redesigned as structured, accountable, meaning-preserving systems-measurable, trainable, and ethically grounded. This is both a philosophy of technology and a field manual for building responsible, life-enhancing human-AI coexistence-where truth, freedom, and happiness can be guarded together, not traded off.
