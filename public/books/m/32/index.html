Book Description

Decoding Originality Dynamics rebuilds originality from the ground up. Instead of treating originality as talent, passion, curiosity, or "critical thinking," it argues that true 0→1 breakthroughs follow a hard generative logic: an old order must first lose its power to explain, organize, or sustain practice — only then can a new order be born. Three markers must appear together: old death and new birth, a new entanglement of features, and the generative role of chaos. Interest, moral ideals, aesthetic admiration, self-realization and curiosity are not denied but repositioned as outcomes or maintenance mechanisms. The only reliable engine of originality is being forced. In the GPT era, the decisive advantage is co-creative originality with GPT: machines accelerate variation and chaos, humans supply crisis-awareness, meaning, and the courage to let the old die.
