中华之气

智慧与原创之根 · The Qi of Chinese Civilization

光让世界被看见，气让世界被感到。

本书主张：「气」不是隐喻，不是迷信，也不只是一个医学名词，而是一套文明逻辑——存在借之变得融贯，语言借之有了温度，创造借之得以连续，社会借之可以安居。当现代文化惯于把世界当作要照亮、丈量、控制之物，本书返回一条更深的原理：世界首先是被呼吸、被感受、被共振的——早在它被定义之前。

四编十八篇，从子宫中的触觉、中国人的一双手，讲到汉语的呼吸与中华的逻辑，再讲到科学、艺术与技术的原创，最后落到文明的复兴：当 AI 能规模化复制知识与逻辑，人类决定性的前沿不再是重复，而是原创。

Book Description

The Qi of Chinese Civilization argues that "Qi" is not a metaphor, not a superstition, and not merely a medical term. It is a civilizational logic: the living rhythm through which existence becomes coherent, language becomes warm, creativity becomes continuous, and society becomes inhabitable. Where modern culture often treats the world as something to be illuminated, measured, and controlled, this book returns to a deeper principle: the world is first breathed, felt, and resonated with — before it is ever defined. Across philosophy, language, art, medicine, ethics, and modern technology, the authors propose a renewed ontology of Qi: not a thing, but a mode of generation; not a static essence, but a dynamic circulation. When AI can replicate knowledge and logic at scale, the decisive human frontier is not repetition but original creation.
