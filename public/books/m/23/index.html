内 容 简 介

在生成式 AI 的时代，最大的危险不是机器能生产语言，而是精致的语言会被错当成真正的思考。本书主张：传统的批判性思维模型——辨识预设、检查逻辑、评估证据、判断结论——已经不够。它历史上虽然珍贵，却仍属于一个「发现范式」的框架：它把预设、逻辑、事实与标准当作早已在那里、有待被找到和评判之物。本书提出一次更深的重构：把「思考」重新定义为理念世界里的一个展开过程。

展 开 · 这本书在讲什么

在此基础上，本书发展出「SDE 批判性思维」，作为 GPT 时代的新核心框架：S 是显露，D 是差异序列，E 是纠缠。（依本学派正名，S 为显露 Show，非「结构」。）本书主张：真正的批判不从裁决开始，而从「揭显」开始——揭示一个思考过程如何被构造、它的路径如何推进、它更深的世界、信息系统与能量如何支撑它；只有到那时，判断才真正开始，不是简单的对错裁定，而是对真、善、美的条件性定位。

与此同时，本书阐明为何「AEI 阅读革命」需要这个更深的批判内核：GPT 阅读、面向 E 的 GPT 阅读、人回到原典的阅读、以及读者—GPT 对话式阅读，可以远不止是阅读工具——在 SDE 批判性思维之下，它们成为一套生成系统，用于更深的理解、更强的判断、读写共生与新的智性创造。这不只是一本讲批判性思维的书，而是一部关于「在 GPT 时代重构思考本身」的著作——横跨阅读、写作、教育、判断、健康、商业与文明。大胆、系统、原创，它为一切拒绝把流畅语言错认成真正思考的人，提供了一套新的智性操作系统。

内 容 简 介 · BOOK DESCRIPTION

In the age of generative AI, the greatest danger is not that machines can produce language, but that polished language can be mistaken for genuine thought. SDE Critical Thinking in the GPT Era argues that the traditional model of critical thinking-identifying assumptions, checking logic, evaluating evidence, and judging conclusions-is no longer enough. While historically valuable, that model still belongs to a discovery-based framework: it treats assumptions, logic, facts, and standards as if they were already there, waiting to be found and judged. This book proposes a deeper reconstruction.

It redefines thinking not as a pile of conclusions, but as an unfolding process in the ideal world. On that basis, it develops SDE critical thinking as a new core framework for the GPT era: S for structure, D for differential sequence, and E for entanglement. Genuine critique, the book argues, does not begin with verdicts. It begins with unveiling-revealing how a thought process is structured, how its path advances, and how its deeper worlds, information systems, and energies sustain it.

Only then can judgment truly begin, not as a simple right-or-wrong decision, but as a conditional positioning of truth, goodness, and beauty. At the same time, the book shows why the AEI Reading Revolution needs this deeper critical core. GPT reading, E-oriented GPT reading, human return-to-the-original reading, and reader-GPT dialogic reading can become far more than reading tools. Under SDE critical thinking, they become a generative system for deeper understanding, stronger judgment, read-write co-generation, and new intellectual creation.

This is not merely a book about critical thinking. It is a work about the reconstruction of thought itself in the GPT era-across reading, writing, education, judgment, health, business, and civilization. Bold, systematic, and original, it offers a new intellectual operating system for anyone who refuses to confuse fluent language with genuine thinking.
