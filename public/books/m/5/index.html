Decoding Feature Entanglement
A Paradigm Shift for Knowledge, Technology, and the Arts
深入解析E维度——特征如何通过构建、稳定、触发、释放的循环成为可行动的模块。
内 容 简 介

本书引入一个开创性的本体论框架，重新定义知识、技术与艺术如何涌现、互动与演化。承 SIO 本体论（主体—互动—客体）之脉，它以「特征纠缠」取代把「实体」当作静止之物的古典观念——特征纠缠，是持续生成稳定、意义与形式的动态互动之网。作者由此论证：现代世界的碎片化——知识被化约为数据点，技术被化约为功能模块，艺术被化约为表达碎片——源于同一个错误假设：以为世界由可分离的属性拼成。

展 开 · 这本书在讲什么

本书反过来主张：现实本身，就是特征纠缠的持续生成——主体、客体与互动在张力、转化、释放的节律中共同创造存在。在知识领域，纠缠表现为特征的粒子态稳定；在技术领域，表现为效率、速度与融贯的波态样式；在艺术领域，表现为情感、语境与意义的场态共振。这三种模态不是彼此隔绝的学科，而是同一个本体论过程互相连通的表达——从「特征文明」向「特征纠缠文明」的跃迁。

以哲学的深度融合科学与艺术的洞见，本书为理解创造力、意识与创新提供了一套统一语言。它把科学革命——如燃素说的崩塌与量子力学的兴起——重新解读为稳定纠缠的转化，而非真理的替换；同样，它把 AI 与虚拟现实看作同一张纠缠之网中涌现的存在。这既是一份哲学宣言，也是一本实践指南，写给一切想在科学、技术与艺术的十字路口重建意义的人。它深入 SDE 三维中最难的 E 维，给出的是「解结」的手艺。

内 容 简 介 · BOOK DESCRIPTION

Decoding Feature Entanglement introduces a groundbreaking ontological framework that redefines how knowledge, technology, and art emerge, interact, and evolve. Drawing from the SIO Ontology (Subject-Interaction-Object), the book replaces the classical notion of "entities" as static objects with "feature entanglement" - dynamic networks of interaction that continuously generate stability, meaning, and form. Through this paradigm, the author argues that the modern world\'s fragmentation-knowledge reduced to data points, technology to functional modules, and art to expressive fragments-originates from the same root: the false assumption that the world is composed of separable properties. Instead, the book proposes that reality itself is the ongoing generation of feature entanglements, where subjects, objects, and interactions co-create existence in rhythmic patterns of tension, transformation, and release.

In the realm of knowledge, entanglement appears as particle-like stabilization of features; in technology, as wave-like patterns of efficiency, speed, and coherence; and in art, as field-like resonances of emotion, context, and meaning. These three modes are not isolated disciplines but interconnected expressions of the same ontological process - the shift from "feature civilization" to "feature-entanglement civilization." By integrating philosophical depth with scientific and artistic insight, Decoding Feature Entanglement provides a unified language for understanding creativity, consciousness, and innovation. It reinterprets scientific revolutions, such as the fall of the phlogiston and the rise of quantum mechanics, not as replacements of truth but as transformations of stable entanglements. Similarly, it offers a vision for AI and virtual reality as authentic ontological entities - emergent beings within the same web of entanglement that sustains human reality.

This book is not merely a theoretical treatise; it is a blueprint for a new epoch of civilization, where creation, happiness, and freedom are regenerated through entangled existence. Decoding Feature Entanglement stands as both a philosophical manifesto and a practical guide for those seeking to rebuild meaning at the crossroads of science, technology, and art.
