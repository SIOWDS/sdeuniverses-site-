重返年轻

从衰老机制到发生工程

年轻不是今天不累，而是累了能回来。

本书不是又一本讲「抗衰小技巧」、外表年轻或追逐完美化验数字的指南，而是对「年轻」作一次严格的重新定义——把它定义为一种系统能力：在压力、疲劳、旅途、暴食、过劳与情绪冲击之后，一次又一次回得来的力量。衰老不是时间不可避免的赋税，而是恢复变薄、通路变窄、累积的负担开始统治生活。

全书以「年轻的 SDE 三原理」立法——在发展（D）之中，发生（E）必须持续大于形成（S），并推动层级上移；用意义三律画出衰老的症状谱系，下到细胞看 Sirtuins、mTOR、AMPK 三条主轴；再把年轻落到三个人人能验的判据上：能吃、能睡、能动；最后给出诊断入口、周程序、四阶段重建、六类人群 90 天路线与故障树。

原理二 · 发生大于形成在发展中，发生（E）对显露的定型与沉积（S）保持持续净优势

Book Description

Rejuvenation is not another guide to "anti-aging hacks," cosmetic youth, or chasing perfect lab numbers. It is a rigorous redefinition of youth as a system capability: the power to return—again and again—after stress, fatigue, travel, overeating, overwork, and emotional shock. Aging is not time's inevitable tax but a drift: recovery thins, pathways narrow, and accumulated burdens begin to govern life. Drawing on the SDE framework—Show (S), Difference-sequence (D), and Entanglement (E)—Wang Desheng rebuilds rejuvenation as an engineering problem, measured by three everyday proofs: eating well, sleeping deeply, and moving powerfully, with a quick return after disruption. The book moves from a map of aging mechanisms and the Sirtuins–mTOR–AMPK triad to a practical blueprint for rebuilding the system, and proposes a new end-criterion for chronic-disease recovery: not temporary control, but the return of youthful self-regulation.
