返青

Rejuvenation
From the Mechanisms of Aging to the Engineering of Genesis
从衰老机制到发生工程——把"变年轻"从愿望还原为可操作的结构重建。（Rejuvenation，ISBN 978-1-970820-48-5）
内 容 简 介

本书不是又一本讲「抗衰小技巧」、外表年轻或追逐完美化验数字的指南，而是对「年轻」作一次严格的重新定义——把它定义为一种系统能力：在压力、疲劳、旅途、暴食、过劳与情绪冲击之后，一次又一次「回得来」的力量。在本书里，「衰老」不是时间不可避免的赋税，而是一种结构性漂移：恢复变薄，通路变窄，累积的负担开始统治生活。

展 开 · 这本书在讲什么

以 SDE 框架——显露（S）、发展性差异序列（D）、涌现性纠缠（E）——为凭（依正名，S 为显露 Show），王德生把「返青」重建为一个工程问题。年轻被当作一个活的「现在时」，不由单一指标衡量，而由三条不肯妥协的日常证据衡量：吃得下（不觉沉重）、睡得深（有真正的修复）、动得有力（快恢复、久留益）。当这些能力一起变强、当身体能在被扰动后迅速「回来」，返青便不再是愿望，而是可重复的现实。

本书从一张衰老机制的深度地图，走向一份重建系统的务实蓝图。它把细胞层面的逻辑（包括 Sirtuins—mTOR—AMPK 三联）与真实世界的运行条件连起来：呼吸、循环、神经系统节律、恢复窗口、以及每周「用力—修复」波型的设计。它还为慢性病康复提出一条新的终点判据：不是暂时的控制，而是「年轻的自我调节」之回归——闭合、修复、以及稳定能力的沉积。一半是哲学，一半是系统科学，一半是野外操作手册，本书给出一个清晰的标准、一套诊断语言、和一条可操作的路径：从衰老的机制，到发生的工程。

内 容 简 介 · BOOK DESCRIPTION

Rejuvenation is not another guide to "anti-aging hacks," cosmetic youth, or chasing perfect lab numbers. It is a rigorous redefinition of youth as a system capability: the power to return-again and again-after stress, fatigue, travel, overeating, overwork, and emotional shock. In this book, "aging" is not time\'s inevitable tax but a structural drift: recovery thins, pathways narrow, and accumulated burdens begin to govern life. Drawing on the SDE framework-Structure (S), Developmental difference-sequences (D), and Emergent entanglement (E)-Wang Desheng rebuilds rejuvenation as an engineering problem.

Youth is treated as a living "present tense," measurable not by a single metric but by three uncompromising everyday proofs: the ability to eat well (without heaviness), sleep deeply (with true restoration), and move powerfully (with fast recovery and lasting gains). When these capacities strengthen together-and when the body can "come back" quickly after disruption-rejuvenation is no longer a wish but a repeatable reality. The book moves from a deep map of aging mechanisms to a practical blueprint for rebuilding the system. It connects cellular logic (including the Sirtuins-mTOR-AMPK triad) with real-world operating conditions: breathing, circulation, nervous-system rhythm, recovery windows, and the design of weekly "wave patterns" of effort and restoration.

It also proposes a new end-criterion for chronic disease recovery: not temporary control, but the return of youthful self-regulation-closure, restoration, and accumulation of stable capability. Part philosophy, part systems science, and part field manual, Rejuvenation offers a clear standard, a diagnostic language, and an actionable path: from the mechanisms of aging to the engineering of genesis.
