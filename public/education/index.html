教育专栏Education

AI 时代，教育不是传递已完成的知识，而是点燃「知识如何发生」。

In the age of AI, education is not the delivery of finished knowledge — it is igniting how knowledge occurs.

专栏筹建中 · 内容陆续上线Column in the making · essays landing soon

当答案唾手可得，教育的旧根基（分数=能力、知识=可传递之物）正在同步松动与硬化。SDE 教育发生学把课堂重新理解为一场发生：混沌激发 · 路径探索 · 冲突整合——三次点火，让学习者不是被灌满，而是被点着。

When answers are one prompt away, education's old foundations — score-as-ability, knowledge-as-transferable-thing — loosen and harden at once. SDE genesis pedagogy re-reads the classroom as an occurrence: chaos ignition, path exploration, conflict integration — three sparks that light the learner rather than fill them.

从「有用」到「不可比」From useful to incomparable

善能被外包，美不能——教育两百年把价值单押在有用上的欠账，正在被 AI 照见。

Goodness can be outsourced; beauty cannot — AI reveals education's two-century bet on usefulness alone.

筛选机器失灵之后When the sorting machine fails

当「分数=能力」被击穿，出口是从可比的分数，转向不可伪造的成长。

Once score-equals-ability breaks, the way out is from comparable scores to un-fakeable growth.

负空间教育学Negative-space pedagogy

模子底下不是废墟，是产房——被代理的「人」消退之处，正是育人重新发生之地。

Beneath the mold is not rubble but a delivery room — where the proxied person fades, the human re-occurs.
