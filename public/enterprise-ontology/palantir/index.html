子栏目立论
THE CLAIM

Palantir 把企业的结构建模、运营、治理到了地表最强——它是秩序态里的企业最优运行智能体。但它止步于「结构」：它造得出最优决策，造不出企业创新；它照得清「企业现在是什么样」，照不见「企业靠什么活着、又该如何在变局中重新发生」。它是一台完美的数字孪生，却不是一台数字发生机。

Palantir models, operates and governs a firm's structure better than anyone — an optimal-operation agent within the order-state. But it stops at structure: it produces optimal decisions, not enterprise innovation; it sees clearly what a firm is now, but not what keeps it alive or how it must re-generate through upheaval. A perfect digital twin — but not a digital genesis engine.
