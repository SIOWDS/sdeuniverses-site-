SDE 语音解析
Voice to SDE
录下来，出文字，再把这番话拆开看。开麦克风或摄像头讲，也可以直接拖进已有的音视频文件——它在你停顿的地方自己切段、切一段转一段，边讲边出字。转写稿再交给 WDS：他到底主张了什么、哪些前提根本没说出口、这番话的 S/D/E 各是什么、缝隙与断链在哪、当场可以问他哪三个问题。说出来的话不能按文章读——观点埋在重复与迂回里，语气强度本身就是信息。三条转写通道任选（本机 Whisper 免费离线／智谱 GLM-ASR／浏览器听写），音视频只在你这台机器上
Record from your mic or camera, or drop in an audio/video file. It cuts at your pauses and transcribes segment by segment as you speak. Then WDS reads the transcript the way speech has to be read: what was actually claimed, which premises were never said out loud, the S/D/E of the talk, where the gaps and broken links are, and three questions you could ask on the spot. Three transcription channels; the media never leaves your machine

Pick three sources — three papers by one author, one each from three authors, three long reads from different fields, three outside theories it finds on the web, or your own paste-ins. They collide pairwise into a judgement none of the three could reach alone, then get written up as a ~20k-character essay. Ten visible stages, each re-runnable and editable; an independent reviewer scores it at the end

Confluence of Disciplines

Give it a question and three disciplines. They collide into a judgement none of the three would accept but their combined evidence forces, written up as a ~20k-character research paper, then audited line by line and rewritten once more. Every source comes with a checkable link

SDE 艺术绘画智能体
SDE Genesis Painter
不是文生图套壳。别处做的是「描述→图像」；这台先答三问——D 这幅画要把什么和什么分开、E 它落在哪一层沉淀里、S 那一刀显露成什么可见形状。三条视觉进路二阶碰撞出典范，近邻闸门滤掉已被占住的图式（群鸟＝涌现、毛线团＝纠缠这类默认图一律作废），出图后基底看着图按「美的九宫格」打分。三档：文章配图／独立画作／开放作画
Not a text-to-image wrapper. It answers three questions first — what does this picture cut apart, which layer of sediment does it land in, and what visible shape does that cut become. Three visual approaches collide into a paradigm; a neighbour gate discards already-occupied imagery; then the base model looks at the result and scores it on the nine-cell grid of beauty.
