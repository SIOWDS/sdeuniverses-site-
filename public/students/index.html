这里陈列 SDE 学员的作品——他们借来一双「事物如何发生」的眼，在各自的学科里，把 SDE 当作隐性的引擎，用目标领域的母语写作。不写「SDE」，却处处是 SDE。

A space for works by SDE trainees — writers who borrow an eye trained on how things come to be, and, in their own fields, use SDE as a hidden engine while writing in the native idiom of their discipline. Never writing “SDE,” yet SDE throughout.

FIRST COHORT · SIGN-UP IN PROGRESS
接龙持续进行，人数不设上限——每一位学员的作品，通过合宪性审读后，都将署名在此陆续发表。
Sign-up is ongoing with no cap on numbers — every trainee’s work, once it passes a constitutionality review, will be published here under their name.
编号按四维加权分自动排序：活跃度 40%（以三天为一个窗口计一次活跃，同一天连发多篇不按篇数叠加；近期权重更高、持续多月额外加分）· 发表篇数 20%（一部专著按十篇论文计）· 创新度 25%（只采用独立盲评分）· 领域广度 15%（一级领域分布的有效领域数）。后两维样本少的向全站均值收缩，暂无数据的按均值计、不罚不奖。每次发表新文章即时更新 · 并列按报名顺序
Ranked by four weighted parts: activity 40% (counted in three-day windows, so a single-day batch does not stack per piece) · number of pieces 20% (a monograph counts as ten papers) · innovation 25% (blind scores only) · disciplinary breadth 15% (effective number of primary fields). The last two are shrunk toward the site mean when the sample is small; students without data take the mean, neither penalised nor rewarded. Updates the instant a new piece is published · ties by sign-up order

WHAT THIS COLUMN COLLECTS

学科本地人的写作
Writing by discipline natives
医学、教育、法律、艺术……学员在自己的领域里落地，用本行的话说本行的事。
Medicine, education, law, art… trainees land SDE in their own fields, saying their field’s things in their field’s words.
带着敬意的解构
Deconstruction with respect
找到一个成见最承重的接缝，看清它怎么拼、哪里拼歪——不抬杠，也不膜拜。
Find the load-bearing seam of a received idea; see how it is joined and where it bends — no quarrel, no worship.
可被推翻的判断
Falsifiable judgments
每一篇都亮出自己可以被推翻的条件，把刀递给读者——这是发生学的诚实。
Every piece states the conditions under which it could be overturned, and hands the knife to the reader — the honesty of generativics.
