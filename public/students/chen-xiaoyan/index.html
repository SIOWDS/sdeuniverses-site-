02简 述 · STATEMENTSTATEMENT

她从生命科学出发，走向生命意义的探索。作为王德生博士终身学员，她系统研修三视角理论、321 理论、SIO / ΔSIO 体系与 SDE 本体论，并将生成性生命观融入健康管理、教育教学与科研实践。

She sets out from the life sciences and moves toward the exploration of life’s meaning. As a lifelong student of Dr. Wang Desheng, she has systematically studied the Three-Perspective theory, the 321 theory, the SIO / ΔSIO framework and SDE ontology, weaving a generative view of life into health management, teaching, and research.

她相信，每一个生命都不是被定义的存在，而是在关系、选择与创造中不断生成的过程。她致力于用科学的严谨、教育的温度和思想的深度，陪伴更多人在生命关系与持续生成中，创造健康、幸福与意义共生的人生。

She believes that no life is a thing to be defined, but a process that keeps generating itself through relationship, choice and creation. With the rigor of science, the warmth of education and the depth of thought, she works to walk alongside others — so that, within the relationships of life and its continual becoming, they may create a life where health, happiness and meaning grow together.
