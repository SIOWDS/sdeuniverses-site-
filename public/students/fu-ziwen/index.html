02自 　 述 · IN HIS OWN WORDSIN HIS OWN WORDS

2019 年，我开始跟随王德生博士学习。此后的几年，我也有幸沿着他的思想发展一路前行：从「三视角智慧」到「321 智慧」，从「主客互动本体论」到「SIO 本体论」，再到今天不断展开的「SDE 本体论」。对我而言，这并不是简单地学习一套已经完成的理论，而是亲历一种思想如何从问题中萌发，在不断追问中突破自身，并逐渐生长出新的理论世界。

也正是在这一过程中，「发生」慢慢成为我理解世界的一种基本方式。我开始重新审视语言、教育、写作、学习、价值以及人的精神生活，并不断追问：那些看似理所当然存在的事物，究竟是怎样一步一步成为今天这个样子的？

近年来，我主要围绕 SDE 本体论及多学科碰撞创新展开研究，希望沿着这条仍在生长的思想道路，继续发现新的问题、新的连接与新的可能。

—— 付 自 文

In 2019 I began to study under Dr. Wang Desheng. In the years since, I have had the good fortune of travelling alongside the development of his thought: from the Three-Perspective Wisdom to the 321 Wisdom, from the Subject–Object Interaction Ontology to the SIO Ontology, and on to the SDE Ontology still unfolding today. For me this was never the study of a finished theory. It was the experience of watching a body of thought germinate out of a problem, break through itself under sustained questioning, and gradually grow a new theoretical world of its own.

It was in that process that genesis slowly became a basic way in which I understand the world. I began to re-examine language, education, writing, learning, value and the life of the spirit, asking again and again: those things that seem to exist as a matter of course — how, step by step, did they come to be what they are today?

In recent years my research has centred on SDE ontology and on innovation through the collision of multiple disciplines, in the hope of following this still-growing path of thought towards new questions, new connections and new possibilities.

— FU ZIWEN
