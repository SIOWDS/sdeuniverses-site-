02简 述 · STATEMENTSTATEMENT

人生上半场，我致力经营企业；人生下半场，我希望经营自己。这些年，我逐渐把探索的重心聚焦于两大主题：健康与财富。健康是创造的根基，却常在拥有时被忽视；财富是自由的保障，却易在追逐中失去方向。我相信，两者都不是人生的目的，而是通向创造、自由与幸福的重要基础。

In the first half of life, I devoted myself to building businesses; in the second half, I hope to cultivate myself. Over these years, I have gradually drawn the focus of my exploration onto two themes: health and wealth. Health is the ground of all creation, yet it is often neglected precisely while we have it; wealth is the guarantor of freedom, yet in the chase for it we easily lose our bearings. I have come to believe that neither is the end of life — both are foundations on the way toward creation, freedom and happiness.

2023 年，有幸跟随王德生老师学习。从三视角、321、SIO，到今天的 SDE，每一步学习都让我不断重新理解生命、生存与生活。而 SDE 发生学，更让我进一步感受到：什么是真正的智慧——也促使我把学习中的思考与实践中的体悟，持续整理成文字。

In 2023 I had the good fortune to study under Mr. Wang Desheng. From the Three Perspectives, 321 and SIO to today's SDE, each step has led me to keep re-understanding life, survival and living. And the genesis view of SDE has let me feel, more deeply still, what real wisdom is — moving me to set down, little by little, the reflections of study and the realizations of practice into words.

愿与更多同行者一起，在探索中成长，在创造中自由，在自由中走向幸福。

May I walk together with more fellow travelers — growing through exploration, made free through creation, and through freedom moving toward happiness.
