PROFILE

张琼

SDE 学员 · 社会学博士
SDE TRAINEE · PhD IN SOCIOLOGY
在 AI 重塑的世界里，打捞人的坐标
In a world reshaped by AI, salvaging the coordinates of the human
欢迎。这里不贩卖答案，只追问正在发生的问题。如果你觉得 AI 写的东西“没味”——那不是文笔问题，是意义问题。请坐，这间客厅，为你而留。
Welcome. This is not a place that sells answers — only one that keeps asking about what is happening. If AI-written text feels “flavorless” to you, that is not a matter of style, but of meaning. Please, take a seat; this living room is kept for you.

我 是 谁

WHO I AM

  • 社会学博士出身——训练我读懂系统如何塑造人。
  • 半路转向应用心理学——学习如何触碰个体的温度。
  • 最终回到传统文化——追问心安何处。
  • A PhD in sociology — trained to read how systems shape people.
  • Turned midway to applied psychology — learning to touch the warmth of the individual.
  • And finally back to traditional culture — asking where the heart finds its peace.

我的 SDE 底层翻转：从“发现”到“发生”。旧坐标不是被我找到的，是正在发生的——我只是去辨认、记录、陪伴它们。

My SDE inversion at the ground: from “discovery” to “genesis.” The coordinates are not found by me but are happening — I only go to recognize them, record them, and keep them company.

最 后 的 话

A LAST WORD

旧坐标在消失，新大陆正从裂缝中生成。我们一起去看看。

The old coordinates are vanishing; a new continent is forming from the cracks. Let us go and see it, together.

—— 张琼 · 2026 年 · 盛夏 · 于开平
— Zhang Qiong · Midsummer 2026 · Kaiping
