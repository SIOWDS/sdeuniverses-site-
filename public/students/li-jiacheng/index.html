01学 员 介 绍 · PROFILEPROFILE

李佳城

教育学硕士 · 基督教传道人 · 《人生的真谛》合作者之一
M.Ed. · Christian minister · Co-author of The True Meaning of Life
SDE 学员 · 教育与生命关怀
SDE TRAINEE · EDUCATION & PASTORAL CARE

长期从事教育、牧养与生命关怀相关工作。多年来，他持续扎根真实的教育与家庭现场，关注不同年龄阶段个体在成长、关系与信仰中的内在需要，尤其重视家庭对生命塑造的基础性作用。他认为，教育并非知识与规范的简单传递，而是一个人被理解、被引导，并在真实关系与生活中逐渐成长的过程。

在系统学习并实践 SDE 发生学体系的过程中，他进一步将多年教育与牧养经验纳入更加清晰的结构之中，持续探索教育、家庭与生命成长背后的发生机制。其为人朴实，重实践而少言说，致力于让思想重新进入真实生活，并在具体关系中承担责任。

A long-time worker in education, pastoral ministry and the care of persons. For years he has stayed close to real classrooms and real families, attending to what individuals of different ages need as they grow — in their relationships and in their faith — and holding that the family is the ground on which a life is shaped. Education, in his view, is not the plain transfer of knowledge and norms, but the slow growth of a person who is understood, guided, and formed inside real relationships and ordinary life.

Through studying and practising the SDE genesis framework, he has brought years of educational and pastoral experience into a clearer structure, and continues to look for the mechanisms by which education, family and the growth of a life actually come about. Plain in manner, longer on practice than on talk, he works to let thought re-enter real life and to carry responsibility within concrete relationships.
