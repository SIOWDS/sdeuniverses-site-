向晴

Abby

英文名 · Abby
向晴 · Xiang Qing
SDE 学员 · 生命成长陪伴者
SDE TRAINEE · COMPANION IN LIFE & GROWTH
在生活、关系、亲子和信仰的路上，一点一点走向晴朗
On the path of life, relationship, parenting and faith — turning, little by little, toward the light
02简 述 · STATEMENTSTATEMENT

“向晴”不是因为生命没有阴天，而是在阴天里，依然选择转向光。曾经，她也习惯用过去的经验、固有的思维与熟悉的方式去应对人生的问题——努力、承担、解决、控制。在 SDE 的学习中，她开始有了新的看见：生命不是重复过去的模式，而是在每一个当下重新觉察、重新选择、重新发生。她学习的，不只是“发现”生命的问题，而是参与生命的“发生”；不只是分析过去，而是在当下创造新的可能。

“Xiang Qing” — turning toward the clear sky — does not mean a life without cloudy days, but choosing, even on cloudy days, to turn toward the light. She once met life’s problems with past experience and habitual thinking: striving, bearing, solving, controlling. Through her study of SDE, a new way of seeing opened up: life is not the repetition of old patterns, but a re-noticing, re-choosing and re-occurring in each present moment. What she learns is not only to “discover” life’s problems, but to take part in life’s “occurring” — not only to analyze the past, but to create new possibility in the present.

多年的家庭、婚姻、亲子、企业经营与生命成长经历，让她越来越相信：每一个生命都值得被看见，每一个关系都有重新连接的可能。「向晴有光」是她记录生命探索、陪伴同行的空间——分享关于家庭、关系、亲子、情绪成长与信仰的思考与实践。愿这些文字像一盏小灯，陪伴正在经历风雨的人：在真实中遇见自己，在关系中学习爱，在每一个当下向光而行。

Years of experience in family, marriage, parenting, running a business and personal growth have led her to a deepening conviction: every life deserves to be seen, and every relationship holds the possibility of reconnection. “Xiang Qing You Guang” — where there is a turn toward clarity, there is light — is her space for recording the exploration of life and walking alongside others, sharing reflections and practice on family, relationship, parenting, emotional growth and faith. May these words be a small lamp for those weathering life’s storms: to meet themselves in what is real, to learn love within relationship, and to move toward the light in every present moment.
