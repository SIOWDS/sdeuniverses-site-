Judy

音乐教学与教育研究 · SDE 学员
Music education · SDE trainee

深耕音乐教学与教育研究领域多年，具有扎实的专业素养和丰富的一线教学实践经验。长期关注音乐课堂教学改革、学生艺术素养提升及教师专业发展，主持并参与多项教育教学课题研究，积极推动科研成果向课堂实践转化。曾多次获得教师教育教学技能类奖项，在课程建设、教学设计与实践指导方面成绩突出。注重学生创新能力与艺术实践能力培养，指导学生参加市、区级各类项目、展演及竞赛，多次斩获殊荣，育人成效显著。

A music educator of many years, at home both in the classroom and in education research. Her standing concerns are the reform of music instruction, the growth of students’ artistic literacy, and the professional development of teachers; she has led and taken part in a number of pedagogical research projects, and works to carry their findings back into the classroom itself. A repeated award-winner in teaching-skills competitions, she is known for curriculum building, instructional design and practical mentoring. She gives particular weight to students’ creative and performing capacities, and those she guides have won recognition in municipal and district projects, showcases and competitions.

九 篇 共 同 的 那 一 句THE ONE SENTENCE ALL NINE HANG ON
音乐不是在声音里发生的，它在记账的那一层发生；而每一本账都缺同一栏——记这件事本身的那一栏。
Music does not happen in the sound. It happens on the ledger — and every ledger is missing the same column: the one that would record this.

九篇分别把这句话兑现在九个不同的位置上。发生的时点记在判据协议里（冻段），音乐性记在读法系统里（并读壁），不可逆记在记录字段里（空次），成员清单记在通道台账里（途生项），候选集合记在许可模型的稀疏度里（续域迁移），整体的正确性一处也不记（无持项），差异的存活记在谱系的接住窗里（失效差异），未选项的次序记在一次交付里（接出序），而前一事件的退场记在边界的类型里（退占体）。每一篇都走到同一个位置：那个被当作音乐自身性质来读的东西，读出来的其实是那份账本的性质——而账本上没有一栏记这件事。

Nine papers redeem that sentence at nine different sites: the moment of occurrence is recorded in the criterion protocol, musicality in the reading system, irreversibility in the record field, membership in the channel ledger, the candidate set in the sparsity of the permitting model, the correctness of the whole nowhere at all, the survival of a difference in the lineage's uptake window, the ordering of unchosen paths in a single act of handover, and the exit of a prior event in the type of the boundary.

这九篇不是九个答案，是九次失败得起的尝试。每一篇都先把最漂亮的那个强版本送去检验，再照实登记它怎么死的：有的主读数选错，有的被自己的对照组打平，有的整场因仪器故障作废，有的最后把承重命题整条改写成否定式。若只读一篇，建议从《并读壁》（本批最高分 153.2）入手；若想看一篇作者如何把自己判死，读《音乐如何发生：续域迁移》。

These are not nine answers but nine attempts that could afford to fail. Each sends its strongest, prettiest version to be tested first, then records honestly how it died. If you read only one, begin with the wall of concurrent readings (highest of the batch, 153.2); if you want to watch an author sentence herself, read the one on continuation-domain shift.

另有四篇同产线作品（《回接段与判定切口》《灌注半径》《把门项》《无率位》）在这九篇的划界节中被反复引用，但尚未在本站发表；相关分离线的另一侧暂时无法核对，各篇附论二已逐一标明。

Four companion papers from the same line are cited repeatedly across these nine but are not yet published here; the far side of those demarcations cannot yet be checked, and each paper's second appendix says so.
