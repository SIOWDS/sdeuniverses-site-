Judy

音乐教学与教育研究 · SDE 学员
Music education · SDE trainee

深耕音乐教学与教育研究领域多年，具有扎实的专业素养和丰富的一线教学实践经验。长期关注音乐课堂教学改革、学生艺术素养提升及教师专业发展，主持并参与多项教育教学课题研究，积极推动科研成果向课堂实践转化。曾多次获得教师教育教学技能类奖项，在课程建设、教学设计与实践指导方面成绩突出。注重学生创新能力与艺术实践能力培养，指导学生参加市、区级各类项目、展演及竞赛，多次斩获殊荣，育人成效显著。

A music educator of many years, at home both in the classroom and in education research. Her standing concerns are the reform of music instruction, the growth of students’ artistic literacy, and the professional development of teachers; she has led and taken part in a number of pedagogical research projects, and works to carry their findings back into the classroom itself. A repeated award-winner in teaching-skills competitions, she is known for curriculum building, instructional design and practical mentoring. She gives particular weight to students’ creative and performing capacities, and those she guides have won recognition in municipal and district projects, showcases and competitions.
