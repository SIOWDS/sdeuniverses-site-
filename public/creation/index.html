学术创造专栏Academic Creation

论文越来越多，能挪动地基的思想越来越少。这不是天才变少了——是思想赖以出生和存活的那几样东西，正被一套没有恶意、只是太有效的制度，一件一件地拿走。

More papers, fewer ideas that move the ground. Not because genius has thinned out, but because the few things a thought needs in order to be born and to stay alive are being taken away, one at a time, by an institution that means no harm and is merely very effective.

FIVE CHANNELS

学术创造的五条战线Five Fronts of Academic Creation

一篇论文有三种命：进期刊、成学位、变典范。它们不是难度递增的三级台阶，而是三套互不兼容的要求——为其中一种写的东西，通常正好不合另外两种。另外两个频道追的是这三种命共同的前提：知识究竟是怎么被生产出来的（科研哲学），以及生产它的人凭什么还创造得动（创造力）。

A paper has three possible fates: enter a journal, become a degree, turn into a paradigm. These are not three rungs of one ladder but three mutually incompatible sets of demands — what is written for one is usually wrong for the other two. The remaining channels pursue what all three presuppose: how knowledge gets produced at all, and what keeps the people producing it still able to create.

五个频道之下，是本专栏原初的四篇长文合辑 —— 壹 · 思想如何出生 · 贰 · 思想如何活下来

Below the channels: this column's original four essays — I · How Thought Is Born · II · How Thought Survives

这个专栏与论文抄袭专栏是同一个问题的两面。那边问：抄袭到底是什么？这边问：真正的创造如何可能？两边最后撞上的是同一堵墙——一套只承认已经凝固为成品的价值、看不见任何仍在生成中的东西的评价制度。抄袭是这堵墙下最刺眼的结算，创造的稀薄是同一堵墙下更安静、也更彻底的那一笔。

This column and the Plagiarism column are two faces of one question. That side asks what plagiarism really is; this side asks how genuine creation is possible. Both run into the same wall: a system of evaluation that recognises value only once it has hardened into a product, and is blind to anything still in the making. Plagiarism is the loudest settlement at the foot of that wall. The thinning of creation is the quieter, more complete one.

壹I 思想如何出生 · 在它还说不清自己是什么的时候How Thought Is Born · While It Still Cannot Say What It Is

一个想法最脆弱的时候，不是它被反驳的时候，是它还没有形状、连想它的人都说不清它是什么的时候。制度在这个时刻要求它说清楚——不是出于恶意，只是因为说不清的东西无法被管理。

A thought is at its most fragile not when it is refuted but when it has no shape yet — when even the person thinking it cannot say what it is. That is the moment the institution asks it to speak clearly. Not out of malice: what cannot be stated cannot be managed.

杂质身份的三种授予机制：论"清除"在不同系统类型中的非同一性Three Modes of Assigning Impurity

半导体先把硅提纯到十一个九，再把杂质精确地放回去；免疫学发现环境过洁的后果不是免疫力下降而是认错人；同一种细菌在胃里致癌、在食管那端却是保护性的。三方对同一动作给出互不相容的评价。本文撤销它们共同的前提：是不是杂质不是被清除物的属性，是一次身份授予的结果——规格授予下清除是减法，遭遇授予下清除同时移除了系统建立分类所需的校准材料，位置授予下清除必然是重排，而接管者尚无名称，故每一轮成功的清除都使下一轮问题更难被命名。含三问判别程序、四例并置演算、六个近邻的硬校验、三项可执行的研究设计与七项可证伪条件。

Semiconductors purify silicon to eleven nines and then put impurity back with precision; immunology finds that over-clean environments do not weaken the immune system but leave it misclassifying; the same bacterium is carcinogenic in the stomach and protective at the oesophagus. Impurity is not a property of the thing removed but an act of identity assignment — and each successful removal lowers the nameability of the next problem.

缠斗生态：学术深层发明发生学的一个追问The Grappling Ecology: A Genetic Inquiry into Deep Invention

不是你朝着一个对象去探索——是一个还没到来的东西在追着你跑，逼你反复改造自己，好给它腾出一个能露面的位置。达尔文的二十二年、维特根斯坦禁止记录的对话、陈景润六平方米的小屋，靠的都不是制度的保护，而是制度还没顾上的角落。而这些角落，正在被一件一件地填平。

You are not exploring an object. Something not yet arrived is chasing you, forcing you to reshape yourself until it has room to appear. Darwin's twenty-two years, Wittgenstein's unrecorded conversations, Chen Jingrun's six square metres — none of them was protected by an institution. Each survived in a corner the institution had not got round to. Those corners are being paved over.

接生思想：学术创造的社会发生机制Midwifing Thought: The Social Genesis of Academic Creation

思想在一个人心里孕育，却必须在他人的手中出生。导师那句"这个太细了，不够撑一个项目"并不刻薄，甚至是负责任的——但它落下的位置，恰好是那团东西还没来得及说出自己是什么的地方。达尔文有胡克和赖尔，那位青年学者只有一个申报书模板。

A thought is conceived in one mind but must be delivered by another's hands. "Too small to carry a grant" is not unkind — it is responsible advice. It simply lands at the exact moment the thing has not yet managed to say what it is. Darwin had Hooker and Lyell. The young scholar had a grant template.

贰II 思想如何活下来 · 出生只是开始How Thought Survives · Birth Is Only the Beginning

一个理论被造出来之后，它靠什么不死？通常的答案是：靠它自带的素质——真、有用、美——被人识别出来。这个答案把最关键的一件事说反了：理论的命，从来不在它自己手里。

Once a theory exists, what keeps it from dying? The usual answer: its own qualities — true, useful, beautiful — get recognised. That answer inverts the crucial fact. A theory's life was never in its own hands.

供养：理论存活的一个被遮蔽条件Sustenance: A Hidden Condition of a Theory's Survival

1854年黎曼讲完，满座没几个人听懂。它没死，是因为此后六十年里，贝尔特拉米、里奇、爱因斯坦各自带着自己的问题从它的裂缝钻进去，用自己的手把它推成了它原本不是的样子。帕森斯的理论没有被驳倒——它是被他自己做完的：每一次把话说尽，都是在拆掉后人能接上去的那个接口。

In 1854 Riemann finished speaking and almost no one had followed. It survived because over the next sixty years Beltrami, Ricci and Einstein each entered through its cracks with problems of their own and pushed it into something it had not been. Parsons was never refuted — he finished himself off: every gap he closed removed a joint a successor could have gripped.

节律剥夺：同质化时钟如何制造认知突变的制度性稀缺Rhythm Deprivation: How a Single Clock Manufactures Cognitive Scarcity

问题从来不在于快。范式内的精细化在快节律里跑得很好。问题在于单一：当所有工作都被塞进同一套以短期可计量性为轴心的时钟，那些需要另一种时间的认知就因为无法在这口钟下证明自己而消失。量子纠缠在成为诺奖之前，当了三十年的"哲学废话"——它活下来，靠的是当年还有那么一块没被这口钟覆盖的地方。

Speed was never the problem; refinement inside a paradigm runs beautifully at speed. Uniformity is. Force every kind of work onto one clock keyed to short-term measurability, and the cognition that needs another kind of time simply vanishes. Entanglement spent thirty years as "philosophical nonsense" before the Nobel — it survived because back then a patch of ground still lay outside that clock.

四篇长文围绕同一个原初问题：真正的学术创造如何可能？四篇各自独立成立，合起来是一条线——被追着跑（缠斗）、被人接住（接生）、被一代代人推着走（供养）、以及这三件事共同需要的那样东西：一段不被时钟切碎的时间（节律剥夺）。

Four essays, one root question: how is genuine academic creation possible? Each stands alone; together they form a line — being chased (grappling), being caught (midwifing), being pushed onward by successive hands (sustenance), and the one thing all three require: time that the clock has not cut to pieces (rhythm deprivation).
