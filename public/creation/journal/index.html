期刊论文Journal Papers

真正的守门人不是审稿人，是「可被审」这件事本身——想法必须先长成可被审的形状，而那个形状正削掉它最锋利的地方。

The gatekeeper was never the reviewer. It is reviewability itself: an idea must first grow into a shape that can be judged — and that shape trims off exactly where it was sharpest.

四问 · 相关长文 2 篇（1 篇今日修订）· 持续增补Four questions · 2 related essays (1 revised today) · Growing

一个想法要进期刊，必须先能被审；要能被审，它就得有边界、有方法、有对照、有可核的结论——也就是说，它必须先凝固。这套要求没有恶意，甚至是文明的：没有它，学术就是一堆各说各话，谁嗓门大谁赢。但它有一笔从不入账的代价：凡是还在生成中、还说不清自己是什么的东西，一律过不了这道门——而那恰好是所有新东西出生时的样子。所以审稿人只是执行者，真正的筛子是可审性本身，它在稿件写下第一行之前就已经在工作了。这个频道不反对期刊，它想弄清楚的是这笔代价具体记在哪儿、由谁付、以及在必须交这笔钱的前提下，还有哪些东西能被夹带进去。

To enter a journal, an idea must first be reviewable; to be reviewable it must have boundaries, a method, controls, a checkable conclusion — that is, it must first harden. The demand carries no malice and is arguably civilised: without it, scholarship is a crowd talking past itself and the loudest wins. But it carries a cost that is never entered in the books: anything still in the making, anything that cannot yet say what it is, fails at the door — and that is what everything new looks like when it is born. The reviewer is only the executor. The real sieve is reviewability itself, and it starts working before the first line is written. This channel is not against journals. It wants to know where the cost is booked, who pays it, and what can still be smuggled through by those who must.

壹I 这个频道要回答的What This Channel Pursues

四问追的是同一台装置的四个部件。它们没有一个是坏的——每一个单看都合情合理，合起来才是那张网。

Four questions tracking four parts of one apparatus. Not one of them is broken; each is entirely reasonable on its own. Only together do they make the net.

  1. 摘要的暴力The Violence of the Abstract「用两百字说清你的贡献」这个要求，本身就在挑选贡献的类型：凡是两百字说不清的贡献，等于不存在。这条规则从不拒绝任何人，它只是让某一类工作在填表阶段自动消失——连被拒的记录都不会留下。"State your contribution in 200 words" does not merely describe a contribution; it selects for a type. Any contribution that cannot be stated in 200 words does not exist. The rule rejects no one. It simply makes a class of work vanish at the form-filling stage, leaving not even a record of rejection.
  2. 三个审稿人互相矛盾，编辑说「综合修改」Three Reviewers Contradict; the Editor Says "Revise Accordingly"这句话在要求什么？它要求的不是更对，是更少可攻击面。你照着改，改出来的东西同时满足了三个互相矛盾的立场——那通常只有一种可能：它不再对任何一个立场构成挑战。修改后的版本更容易通过，也更没有必要存在。What is being asked? Not for the paper to be more right, but for it to present less surface to attack. Comply, and you produce something that satisfies three mutually contradictory positions at once — which usually means one thing: it no longer challenges any of them. The revised version passes more easily, and needs to exist less.
  3. 影响因子的回写The Impact Factor Writes Back你以为自己在选投哪里，其实是在被选写什么。期刊的位置反过来决定了什么算问题——一个题目「有没有出口」在动笔之前就参与了选题，而这道回写是无声的：没有人下过命令，每个人都只是理性。You believe you are choosing where to submit; you are being chosen for what to write. A journal's standing decides, in reverse, what counts as a problem — whether a topic "has an outlet" enters the choice of topic before the first line is written. The feedback is silent: no one issued an order, everyone was merely being rational.
  4. 拒绝的社会生产The Social Production of Rejection评审装置的社会产物不是准确，是让拒绝变得无可争辩。这不是阴谋——一个必须每年淘汰九成来稿的系统，最紧要的事从来不是选对，是选得让人没话说。测度就是它为此分泌出来的那层壳。The social product of a review apparatus is not accuracy but rejection made unarguable. This is not a conspiracy: for a system that must turn away nine in ten submissions each year, the pressing task was never to choose rightly but to choose in a way that leaves nothing to say. Measurement is the shell it secretes for that purpose.

两篇都不是关于期刊的，但都是关于同一台装置的——一篇拆它怎么分泌壳，一篇拆它怎么把管不了的东西抵押出去。第一篇的修订稿把第四问（拒绝的社会生产）直接推到了本频道自己身上：同行评审是那条律的第六个兑现，而它是唯一一个作者自己身处其中的。

Neither is about journals; both are about the same apparatus — one takes apart how it secretes shells, the other how it mortgages what it cannot govern. The revised first essay turns the fourth question (the social production of rejection) directly on this channel: peer review is the law's sixth instantiation, and the only one its author is standing inside.

拒绝的社会生产：教育评价的测度外壳与外壳再分泌律The Manufacture of Rejection: The Measurement Shell and the Law of Shell Re-secretion

评价装置生产的不是关于学习的信息，是可接受的拒绝；测度是它分泌出来的壳。拆掉一层壳，装置立刻分泌下一层——USMLE Step 1 改为通过/不通过之后，压力原样搬到了 Step 2。修订稿补上了脱钩的时间机制：壳与被测对象脱钩得多快，由优化产业成熟得多快决定；人工智能不是揭穿了壳，是把再分泌的周期从两代人压到一个学年——于是壳换代而不消亡，从结果的不可争辩换到过程的不可争辩。期刊评审不是同一台装置的另一个物种，是同一台装置：双盲评审与科举糊名在结构上是同一样东西，理由也完全一致——它不提高判断的准确性，它消除可指认的施害者。预注册、数据强制公开、写作留痕，正在被读为科研诚信；本文读为换代。文末解剖了一片标本：本文自己为了在这台装置里活下来而分泌的那个式子。

An assessment apparatus does not produce information about learning; it produces acceptable rejection, and measurement is the shell it secretes. Strip one shell and the next is secreted at once — when USMLE Step 1 went pass/fail, the pressure moved intact to Step 2. The revision supplies the timing mechanism: how fast a shell decouples is set by how fast its optimisation industry matures, and AI has compressed that cycle from two generations to a single academic year — so the shell changes generation rather than dying, from unarguable results to unarguable process. Journal review is not another species of the same apparatus; it is the apparatus: double-blind review and the sealed names of the imperial examinations are structurally one thing, for identical reasons — neither improves the accuracy of judgement; both remove anyone who can be named as the culprit. Preregistration, mandatory data release and writing trails are read as research integrity; this paper reads them as the change of generation. It closes by dissecting one specimen: the formula this paper itself secreted in order to survive that apparatus.

原创抵押权：制度化知识生产中原创定义的自我取消The Originality Mortgage: How Institutional Knowledge Production Cancels Its Own Definition of Originality

制度把它管不了的认知发生，抵押给它管得了的产权标记——查重率、创新点声明、期刊级别。抄袭不是道德溃疡，是这笔抵押契约在最薄弱节点上的极端兑现。

The institution mortgages the cognitive event it cannot govern against the property markers it can: similarity scores, novelty statements, journal tiers. Plagiarism is not a moral ulcer but the extreme redemption of that mortgage at its weakest node.
