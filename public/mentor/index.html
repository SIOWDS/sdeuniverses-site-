导师师范文

The Mentor’s Model Essays

这里是王德生亲手写的范文——给学员当样板。同一双「事物如何发生」的眼，落到哲学、国学、红学、健康、教育、语言、艺术里，每一篇都为一件事立个样板：如何在一门具体学科里，把 SDE 当作隐性的引擎，用本行的母语写作。不写「SDE」，却处处是 SDE。先看导师怎么落地，再去学员专栏动手写自己的。

Model essays written by the mentor, Wang Desheng — made to be studied and imitated. One eye, trained on how things come to be, lands in philosophy, the Chinese classics, redology, health, education, language and art. Each piece demonstrates a single move: how to make SDE a hidden engine inside one real discipline, writing in that field’s native idiom. Never writing “SDE,” yet SDE throughout. Watch the mentor land it — then write your own in the Students’ Column.

哲学 · 解构思想史的巨人
Philosophy · Deconstructing the giants
先把对手扶到最强，再指出他那记重拳，偏了几度。
First raise the opponent to his strongest, then show by how many degrees his punch missed.
康德的范畴，不是先天的模具，而是沉淀的纠缠
Kant’s categories are not an a-priori mold, but sedimented entanglement
师法：把最硬的「先天／先验」成见，还原成「在什么条件下沉淀出来」。
Demonstrates: taking the hardest “a-priori” assumption and reducing it to “under what conditions it sedimented.”

这些范文，怎么用

HOW TO USE THESE MODELS

先挑最近的一篇
Start with the nearest one
找离你本行最近的学科，读它，不是读观点，是读它怎么动手：从哪里下刀、怎么翻转、在哪里收。
Find the discipline closest to your field and read it — not for the thesis, but for the moves: where it cuts, how it flips, where it lands.
拆开看它的骨架
Take the skeleton apart
每篇都有同一副骨架：找接缝 → 把「如何被发现」翻回「如何发生」→ 亮出可被推翻的条件。学骨架，不抄血肉。
Every piece shares one skeleton: find the seam → turn “how discovered” into “how it arises” → state the falsifiable condition. Learn the skeleton, not the flesh.
换你的学科重写一遍
Rewrite it in your field
同一副骨架，换你自己领域的血肉，用本行的话说本行的事——然后投到学员专栏。
Same skeleton, your field’s flesh, your field’s words for your field’s things — then submit to the Students’ Column.
