壹I 抄袭的发生学 · 它为何发生，又为何抓不住The Genesis of Plagiarism · Why It Happens and Why It Escapes

把抄袭当成道德污点，就只能靠事后追责；把它当成一种可分析的发生过程，才能看清它在哪一道工序上必然发生——而那道工序，恰恰是查重软件永远看不见的地方。

Treat plagiarism as a moral stain and you can only punish it afterwards. Treat it as an analysable process and you can see the exact step at which it becomes inevitable — the step no similarity checker can see.

抄袭不是被道德放行的，是被一道没人负责去问的程序缝隙放行的。匿名评审只在一个方向上真正匿名——审稿人不知道作者；更致命的另一个方向是：审稿人无法知道作者是否真的做过。

Plagiarism is not waved through by morals but by a procedural gap nobody owns. Blind review is blind in only one direction — and no stage is ever assigned the question of whether the signer did the work.

不知道自己有前辈，算不算抄？If You Never Knew the Field Existed, Is It Still Plagiarism?

一个人独立想出了别人三十年前想出的东西，还以为自己是第一个开口的——这在学术上是什么？不是道德问题，是工序问题：它暴露的不是人品，是这个人从未做过对手辨识。而后果与抄袭相同：读者无从知道他多说了什么。

Someone independently arrives at what a field settled thirty years ago, believing they spoke first. This is not a moral failure but a missing step — and its consequence matches plagiarism: no reader can tell what was added.

洗稿的语法：为什么改写比照抄更难被抓住，也更该被抓住The Grammar of Laundering: Why Paraphrase Is Harder to Catch and Worse

查重软件比对的是字符串，而洗稿改的正是字符串。于是出现一个荒谬的分工：机器抓得住最笨的抄袭，抓不住最狡猾的。真正的判据不在字面重合度，在论证骨架的同构——而骨架无法被查重，只能被读出来。

Similarity software compares strings; laundering rewrites strings. The result is an absurd division of labour: machines catch the clumsiest theft and miss the cleverest. The real criterion is isomorphism of argumentative skeleton — which no checker sees, only a reader does.

贰II 原创的判据 · 它能不能被别人算出来Criteria of Originality · Can Anyone Else Compute It?

「我觉得这是原创的」是自陈，不是判据。判据必须能被别人拿去算，且算出来可能是你不想要的答案——一个不会给出坏消息的原创标准，就不是标准。

"I feel this is original" is self-report, not a criterion. A criterion must be computable by someone else, and must be able to return an answer you did not want — a standard that never brings bad news is not a standard.

原创的判据 · 约万字Criteria of Originality · ~10k words

原创是一道减法：你比最近的邻居多说了什么Originality Is a Subtraction: What Did You Add to Your Nearest Neighbour?

原创不是「我的想法是新的」，那是自陈。原创是一道可以被别人算的减法：找出离你最近的那个已有立场，说清你比他多出的那一寸——多不出来，这篇就不该存在。这道减法之所以少有人做，是因为它经常给出你不想要的答案。

Originality is not the claim that your idea is new — that is self-report. It is a subtraction anyone can compute: name your nearest existing position and state the inch you add. If there is no inch, the paper should not exist. Few run this subtraction because it often returns an unwelcome answer.

原创的判据 · 约万字Criteria of Originality · ~10k words

引用最强的对手，而不是最好打的那个Cite Your Strongest Opponent, Not the Easiest One

一篇论文最能暴露作者诚意的地方，是它选择跟谁交手。挑一个稻草人来打，读者立刻知道这篇没底气；正面写出「这条界线很窄，我欠他机制」，读者反而开始相信你。稻草人不是修辞失误，是一种可被检测的心虚。

Nothing exposes a paper faster than whom it picks a fight with. A straw man tells the reader there is no confidence here; admitting that the line is narrow and that you owe your opponent the mechanism is what makes you credible.

谁写的，为什么是一个事实问题，而不是礼节问题Authorship Is a Question of Fact, Not Courtesy

AI 时代让署名问题从礼节退回到事实：一篇文章署谁的名，是关于某个人认知能力的一句陈述，可真可假。给自己写的东西打分、给别人的名字挂自己的产出，都不是失礼，是伪证——而一旦伪证被接受，同一套体系里其余所有的评价就同时失效。

The AI era pushes authorship back from etiquette to fact: a byline is a claim about a person's cognition, and claims can be false. Scoring your own text, or signing your output with another's name, is not rudeness but fabricated evidence — and once accepted, every other assessment in the same system goes void.
