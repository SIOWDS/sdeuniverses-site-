注册入口

Register

站上没有自助开户这回事。注册＝申请进入学员名录——名字进了名录，你才登得进社区、留得下言、交得了稿。表格由王德生本人过目，通过后我们用你留的联系方式把名字与口令发给你。

There is no self-service sign-up here. Registering means applying to join the roster — only a name on the roster can post in the community, comment on papers, and submit work. Every application is read by Dr. Wang himself; if it passes, we send your name and passphrase to the contact you leave below.

进了名录，你能做这四件事

WHAT THE ROSTER OPENS

在 SDE 社区发言——群聊与私聊，用名字加口令登录，不必有 Google 账号。Post in the SDE community — groups and direct messages, with a name and passphrase; no Google account needed.
在任意一篇文章下留言——全站九百多页文章的讨论区都对名录开放。Comment under any paper — the discussion section on every page is open to the roster.
向投稿箱交论文——通过审读的稿子会以你的名字署名发表。Submit papers — work that passes review is published under your own name.
开一个自己的学员专栏——发够篇数后，你在学员专栏里有独立的一页。Open your own column — once you have enough published work, you get your own page in the Students' Column.
名字与联系方式必填，其余可留空。Name and contact are required; the rest is optional.
申请只存进私有收件箱，不公开、不外传，也不会拿去发广告。Applications go into a private inbox only — never published, shared, or used for marketing.
审看要花几天。这几天里站上的文章照样全部可读，不必等。Review takes a few days. Everything published on the site stays readable meanwhile — no need to wait.
